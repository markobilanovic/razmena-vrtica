# Product Overview

**Razmena Vrtica** (Kindergarten Exchange) is a platform for exchanging kindergarten spots in Serbia. The application helps parents find and swap places in kindergartens to better suit their location or needs.

## Core Features

- **Multi-way circular swaps**: Supports complex exchanges (A→B→C→A) beyond simple 1:1 trades
- **User management**: Parent accounts with email authentication
- **Kindergarten database**: Comprehensive list of kindergartens in Serbia
- **Child registration**: Children linked to specific kindergarten spots
- **Wishlist system**: Parents can specify desired kindergartens for each child
- **Matching algorithm**: Automated detection of possible swap cycles
- **Contact system**: Communication between matched parents (planned)

## Business Logic

The platform focuses on solving the common problem where parents have kindergarten spots in inconvenient locations and want to exchange with others for spots closer to home or work. The system supports complex multi-party exchanges, not just simple swaps.

## Target Users

Parents in Serbia who have children enrolled in kindergartens and want to exchange spots for more convenient locations.
